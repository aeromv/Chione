What's inside

Built for competitive play

Every feature is designed around real PvP scenarios. No bloat, no gimmicks.

Locked CPS
Set 13, get 13. No random spikes to 14 or drops to 12. Precision timing with zero jitter.
Right Click Filters
Ignore while shifting, ignore while attacking, allow shift-click in menus. Full control over when right click fires.
AutoSprint
Fixed and reliable. No more random walking when you double-tap W. Works flawlessly on every client.
SprintReset
W-Tap, S-Tap, or Crouch. Only activates during fights, stays off when you're just running around.
Strafing
Fast A/D switching during combat. Won't interfere when you're steering manually.
Any Keyboard
Hotkeys work on Arabic, French, AZERTY, and any other layout. Scan-code based detection.
